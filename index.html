Project Ideas

For this course's final project, she is interested in topics that revolve around critically analyzing AI, especially from a humanistic point of view. Some broad possible ideas are understanding how various religious institutions approach the concept or usage of AI, how AI has affected the lifestyles/outputs of musicians or artists, and in what ways has AI increased or affected the untrustworthiness of politicians from the point of view of "public perception."
